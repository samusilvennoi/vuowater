What VUO is

VUO is a Finnish water brand. Spring water in a 0.5-litre bottle with a body made from wood fibre.

The goal is to reduce plastic bottles. VUO is still in development and not yet on sale.

Why a wood bottle?

Plastic bottles end up in the sea every year.

Up to an estimated34billion plastic bottles a year
  • About 1,000 bottles every second

    That is about 65,000 bottles a minute.

    Calculated from the estimate that up to 34 billion bottles enter the sea each year. Source: Oceana (2020)

  • About 450 years

    That is how long a single plastic bottle is estimated to last in the sea. It does not disappear. It breaks into smaller and smaller pieces.

    Source: World Economic Forum (2018), data from NOAA and Woods Hole Sea Grant

VUO's answer

A body made from wood

The body of the bottle is wood fibre, a renewable raw material.

Recycled with paper and cardboard

The goal is for the bottle to be recycled with paper and cardboard.

One for one

Every VUO replaces one plastic bottle.
